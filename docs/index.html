Simplispace is a Windows desktop app that groups your Gmail by the service that sent it, scores each sender, explains why, and helps you unsubscribe from the ones you don’t need.

Download for Windows

Every sender, scored

Each service gets a 0–100 score and a category (Important, Useful, Optional, Marketing) from clear, explained rules.

Unsubscribe only when you say so

Nothing happens automatically. You pick a sender and confirm how to unsubscribe, and the app checks it worked.

Inbox health

See how much of your mail is worth reading, and how much time unsubscribing saves you each year.

Private by design

Read-only Gmail access. Simplispace reads email details, never message bodies, and keeps everything on your own computer.

How Simplispace uses your Google data

Simplispace asks Google for read-only access to Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It uses that access only to read each email’s sender, subject, date, labels and unsubscribe header, so it can group your mail by sender, score each sender, and offer unsubscribe options. It never reads or stores the content of your emails, never sends or deletes email, and never sends your data to the developer or anyone else. Everything is stored on your computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Simplispace’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
