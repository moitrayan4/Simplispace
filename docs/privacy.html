Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Simplispace (“the app”, “we”) is a Windows desktop application that helps you understand and reduce unwanted email in your Gmail account. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

In short: Simplispace has read-only access to Gmail, reads email details but never message bodies, keeps everything on your own computer, and never sends your data to us or sells it to anyone.

1. Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google, you grant Simplispace the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly permission. With it, the app reads:

  • Received emails: the sender’s name and address, subject line, date, Gmail labels (for example Unread or Promotions), and the List-Unsubscribe header that senders include for unsubscribing.
  • Emails you sent: only the recipients’ addresses (To and Cc), to recognise services you have written to. Only the recipients’ web domains are kept.
  • Your Gmail address, to show which account is connected.

Simplispace does not read, download or store the body or attachments of any email. It cannot send, delete or change your email.

2. How we use this data

The data is used only to provide the app’s features to you:

  • group your email by the service that sent it and show how many emails each one sends, how often you open them, and when the last one arrived;
  • score each sender and explain why, using the subject lines, labels and the other details above;
  • calculate your inbox health score and estimated time saved;
  • offer ways to unsubscribe, and check afterwards whether the sender stopped emailing you.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, do not let anyone read it, and do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

3. Where your data is stored

All data stays on your computer, in the app’s folder for your Windows user (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Simplispace). Your Google sign-in token is encrypted with a key generated on your computer. Simplispace has no servers: the developer never receives your email data, your sign-in, or your choices.

4. When data leaves your computer

The app connects to other services only in these cases:

  • Google: to sign you in and to read the Gmail data described above, through Google’s official Gmail API.
  • Sender logos (optional): to show a sender’s logo, the app looks up that sender’s website address (for example netflix.com, never anything from your emails) once, using Google’s or DuckDuckGo’s website-icon service or the sender’s own website, and saves the logo on your computer. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Unsubscribing, only when you confirm it: depending on the method you choose, the app sends the sender’s standard unsubscribe request to the address the sender put in their email, opens the sender’s unsubscribe page, or opens Gmail in an app window so you can use Gmail’s own Unsubscribe or Block button or send an unsubscribe email yourself.

5. Sharing

We do not share, transfer, rent or sell your Google user data or any other personal data with anyone. The only data that leaves your computer is described in section 4, and it is sent only to complete an action you requested.

6. Limited Use disclosure

Simplispace’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Log out in the app revokes Simplispace’s access to your Google account and deletes all data the app stored for that account.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Simplispace folder removes everything the app has stored.

Data is kept on your computer only until you log out or delete it.

8. Security

Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted, the app only requests read-only access, and the app’s local service accepts requests only from the app itself.

9. Children

Simplispace is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, we will ask for your consent in the app first.
