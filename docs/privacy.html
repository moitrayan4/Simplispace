Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Simplispace (“the app”, “we”) is a Windows desktop application that helps you understand and reduce unwanted email in your Gmail account. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

In short: Simplispace has read-only access to Gmail unless you choose to let it send unsubscribe emails, reads email details but never message bodies, and keeps data on your computer by default. If you turn on AI second opinions and ask for one, a few details about that sender are sent for analysis, as described in section 4.

1. Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google, you grant Simplispace the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly permission. With it, the app reads:

  • Received emails: the sender’s name and address, subject line, date, Gmail labels (for example Unread or Promotions), and the List-Unsubscribe header that senders include for unsubscribing.
  • Emails you sent: only the recipients’ addresses (To and Cc), to recognise services you have written to. Only the recipients’ web domains are kept.
  • Your Gmail address, to show which account is connected.

Simplispace does not read, download or store the body or attachments of any email. It cannot send, delete or change your email.

2. How we use this data

The data is used only to provide the app’s features to you:

  • group your email by the service that sent it and show how many emails each one sends, how often you open them, and when the last one arrived;
  • score each sender and explain why, using the subject lines, labels and the other details above;
  • if you enable AI in Settings, request a second opinion for ambiguous senders;
  • calculate your inbox health score and estimated time saved;
  • offer ways to unsubscribe, and check afterwards whether the sender stopped emailing you.

Simplispace does not use your data for advertising and does not sell it. Simplispace does not use Google user data, including Google Workspace API data, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. AI is off by default. When you turn it on and ask for a second opinion, the fields described in section 4 are used only to return a category, a confidence score and an explanation.

3. Where your data is stored

App data is stored on your computer, in the app’s folder for your Windows user (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Simplispace). Your Google sign-in token is encrypted with a key generated on your computer. Your emails and your choices are not stored on any Simplispace server. The only server is the optional AI server described in section 4, which does not store the details it is sent about a sender.

4. When data leaves your computer

The app connects to other services only in these cases:

  • Google: to sign you in and to read the Gmail data described above, through Google’s official Gmail API.
  • AI second opinions (optional, off by default): only after you turn AI on in Settings and ask for a second opinion about a sender.
    • Simplispace AI server: the app sends that sender’s name, domain and numbers (such as how many emails it sent and how many you opened), with a Google ID token that identifies your Google account so limits can be applied. The token cannot be used to read your mail. The server, hosted on Cloudflare, looks up the company on its website and Wikipedia using its domain and name only, then passes the sender, the numbers and what it found, not your identity, to the AI provider Groq. It does not store or log them. To enforce usage limits it keeps a counter under a scrambled form of your account ID, deleted two days after your last request. Subject lines are not sent.
    • Your own AI key: if you set up your own provider key, the same details and up to three recent subject lines go directly to that provider instead. Subject lines may contain personal information.
    Email contents, attachments and Gmail access are never sent. Cloudflare, Groq and any provider you choose handle data under their own terms. You can turn AI off in Settings.
  • Unsubscribe emails (optional): for a sender with no unsubscribe option, you can ask AI to write an unsubscribe email. That sender’s name, domain, email count and the number of days since its last email go to the AI in the same way, and nothing about you is included. Sending it needs the extra https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send permission, which is requested only when you first use this. The email, which includes your address so you can be removed, is sent from your Gmail to the sender only after you check it and press Send. Some senders instead publish an address for unsubscribing; when you choose to unsubscribe from one of them by email, including as part of a bulk unsubscribe, the app sends a short standard message to that address from your Gmail. The app sends nothing else. To see whether an unsubscribe email worked, the app looks for it in your Sent mail and for replies to it, reading the replies’ subject, technical headers and the one-line preview Gmail provides. Those previews are used on your computer only and are not stored or sent to anyone.
  • Sender logos (optional): to show a sender’s logo, the app looks up that sender’s website address (for example netflix.com, never anything from your emails) once, using Google’s or DuckDuckGo’s website-icon service or the sender’s own website, and saves the logo on your computer. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Unsubscribing, only when you confirm it: depending on the method you choose, the app sends the sender’s standard unsubscribe request to the address the sender put in their email, opens the sender’s unsubscribe page, or opens Gmail in an app window so you can use Gmail’s own Unsubscribe or Block button or send an unsubscribe email yourself.

5. Sharing

Simplispace does not sell your Google user data. Data leaves your computer only for the services listed in section 4 and only to provide the feature you requested. If you use AI second opinions, the services described in section 4 receive the fields listed there.

6. Limited Use disclosure

Simplispace’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Log out clears the saved sign-in and attempts to revoke Simplispace’s Google access. Sender analysis and choices remain on this computer so they are available if you sign in again.
  • Delete account in Settings erases the account’s locally stored data and attempts to revoke Google access.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Simplispace folder removes everything the app has stored.

Data stays on your computer until you delete the account in Settings or remove the app’s data folder.

8. Security

Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted, the app requests read-only access unless you choose to allow sending, and the app’s local service accepts requests only from the app itself.

9. Children

Simplispace is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, we will ask for your consent in the app first.
